# Referee Items 68--70

The correction previously covered only uncertain ESMFold singletons even though the same
calibration measured a high singleton rate among confident relatives. That omission made
the supply estimate favourable by default. The Referee now applies each measured rate to
its measured singleton population. No claim from this stage is admissible until the runtime
boundary holds.

At TM .40/.50/.60 the retained cluster files contain 270/357/404 singletons: 139/175/184
low-confidence ESMFold members and 131/182/220 other members. The corresponding point
corrections produce 238.4/145.2/93.0 clusters, or 47/29/18 panels. At frozen TM .50,
the point estimate closes supply below N_min=42; its independent Wilson-rate sensitivity
is 85.2--250.1 clusters (17--50 panels), which spans close and hold but never proceed.

The confident 6/11 baseline is treated as structural-instrument failure, not demonstrated
biological separation. Only 95/413 ESMFold predictions meet mean pLDDT >=.70, and even
the better sequence relatives split more than half the time. Leaving 182 confident
singletons uncorrected would therefore have no evidential basis and would favour
continuation. This ruling makes effect detection harder.

Item 69 found one material preregistration defect: Item 51 named a rate and threshold
but not the population to which it applied. The audit found no other material unnamed-
population rate, threshold, or correction defect in the 37-element ledger at audit time.
