# Referee Items 65--67

The calibration demonstrates a wide corrected-supply sensitivity range, but it does not
meet the actual preregistered Item-48 criterion. The retained Item 51 text says the
false-singleton-rate Wilson bound must be below .8571; it does not specify subtracting
the confident baseline. Treating the more favourable post-measurement baseline-subtracted
bound as the criterion would change the estimator after seeing it. The hold therefore
remains. No claim from this stage is admissible until the runtime boundary holds.

## Item 65 — Item 48

Verified frozen-TM-.50 values are: low false singleton 8/9, Wilson upper .980109;
confident baseline 6/11 = .545455; baseline-subtracted point excess .343434 and
conservative upper .700018; corrected supply 277.5--400 clusters, 55--80 panels.
The `.700018` result is a useful sensitivity analysis, but it is not the preregistered
rate named in Item 51. Item 48 is **not satisfied**; Gate 1 cannot proceed on structural
supply.

This is deliberately conservative. The calibration has nine low members from seven
clusters; seven of the nine are from sister smut genera, and only one is canonical
despite 47 canonical singleton targets. A taxon- and stratum-balanced calibration could
raise the excess because the current identity-relative relation is easier to recover
than a fold-family relation. Using its corrected range to proceed would therefore be
optimistic.

## Item 66 — structural evidence quality

| Mean pLDDT band | Predictions | Fraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| <.50 | 189 | 45.8% |
| .50--<.70 | 129 | 31.2% |
| .70--<.90 | 81 | 19.6% |
| >=.90 | 14 | 3.4% |
| >=.70 total | 95 | 23.0% |

Under the conventional interpretation, 189 models indicate likely disorder and 129
are low confidence. The 6/11 confident-relative false-singleton baseline also shows
that the channel splits close relatives even before the low-confidence marginal effect
is considered. Structural evidence is consequently noisy for per-item use. This affects
T2.4's measured channel count and any claim about per-item structural evidence. It does
not change the Item-65 supply hold: that ruling is based on the raw preregistered
false-singleton criterion, not a claim that the channel is reliable.

## Item 67 — preregistered channel expectation

Before T2.4 ablation, expect the structural channel to add no reliable contribution to
a fixed mechanical ensemble. It fails if removal is equivalent on the prespecified
primary metric, improves it, or cannot be computed for a material subset because of its
600-residue, non-standard-residue, or reliability limits. This is an expectation, not
an observed result.
